# MEMORANDUM

**TO:** Portfolio Manager (PM) & Investment Committee  
**FROM:** Quantitative Research Team  
**DATE:** August 2026  
**SUBJECT:** Stage 01 Scoping: Evaluation Framework for ETF Strategy Viability  

### Executive Summary
This memorandum outlines the problem scoping, operational constraints, and analytical roadmap for evaluating whether our current ETF momentum strategy will remain profitable next year. 

### Problem & Strategic Objective
Our primary objective is to evaluate out-of-sample strategy persistence and identify regime vulnerabilities before capital rebalancing. 

### Key Metrics & Decision Triggers
1. **Target Out-of-Sample Sharpe Ratio:** > 0.8
2. **Max Tolerable Drawdown:** < 15%
3. **Action Threshold:** If predicted Sharpe drops below 0.5 under macro stress scenarios, we recommend reducing strategy allocation by 50% or transitioning to cash-defensive assets.

### Risk & Assumption Matrix
- **Assumptions:** Execution costs remain within 10-15 bps; market depth supports up to $50M AUM.
- **Risks:** Macro interest rate pivots, factor degradation, liquidity shifts.
